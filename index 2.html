Our Mission

We carry more than boxes.

We are passionate about moving because we believe it is about more than getting your possessions from one place to another. Finding a new home is one of the most profound experiences in a person's life, and we want to be part of that moment with care, respect, and pride in our work.

We started this company because we have lived this work. We were in the moving business for years before founding Dove Hands Movers at 24, fresh out of college, and we built it the way we always believed a moving company should be run.
